By downloading or using Pulse you agree to these terms.

01The app

Pulse is a journal: it keeps the entries, moods, photos, voice notes and places you add, and shows them back to you as a timeline, calendar, insights and reminders. It is provided free of charge.

02Your journal is yours, and so is keeping it safe

Everything you add stays on your device, and you are responsible for it. Pulse has no cloud copy: if you lose or reset your phone, or remove the app, your journal goes with it unless you have exported a backup. Export backups regularly and keep them somewhere safe.

03Insights are not advice

Moods, streaks and recaps reflect what you entered. They are not medical or psychological advice. If you are struggling, please talk to someone you trust or a professional.

04Maps and place search

Maps and place suggestions come from OpenStreetMap and its contributors, under the Open Database License. They can be incomplete or wrong.
